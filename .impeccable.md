## Design Context

### Users
People learning how backends work from zero: curious, often non-technical at the start, reading long-form lessons on a laptop or phone, usually alone and unhurried. On the Math Behind Tech shelf they are following arithmetic line by line and want to check it with a pencil.

### Brand Personality
Patient, hand-set, unhurried. The site is a printed manual whose colours are sampled from one photograph, Mount Fuji from Lake Kawaguchiko in first snow: the sky is the paper (#cfdcec), the mountain's shadow is the ink (#16212e), the mountain blue is the accent (#224e7a). Nothing warm; the whole sheet is the picture's blues. Three words: careful, printed, plain-spoken. It should feel like a well-set textbook, not a product.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial print. Serif display and body (Playfair Display, Source Serif 4), mono for figures and small labels (JetBrains Mono). Light theme by default because it is read like a book; dark theme is the lake at dusk, same blues inverted, with the sky as the accent. The hub carries the photograph itself, pinned behind sections of paper that are a tenth see-through; lesson pages are a plain sheet.

Worked examples are textbook boxed examples: a hairline frame on the same paper, the example number in the margin, the working as aligned lines of arithmetic with the result in bold. Theory and examples are separated by structure, not by colour fills or shadows.

Anti-references: a SaaS dashboard (cards on cards, offset or drop shadows, pill badges, coloured side stripes, icon-above-heading tiles).

### Design Principles
1. Structure separates, colour does not. Frames, rules, numbering and type do the work; no fills, no shadows, no accent stripes.
2. The accent is for reading, not decorating. Links, results, section labels, and the marks. Everything else is ink.
3. Figures are set like a maths book: mono, tabular, right-aligned results, one idea per row.
4. Square corners and hairlines. Nothing floats above the paper.
5. Every element must survive at 420px without becoming a different design.
